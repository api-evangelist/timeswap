# Timeswap

## Timeswap

- [What is Timeswap?](https://timeswap.gitbook.io/docs/introduction/introduction.md): An overview of what Timeswap is, its key features, and a quick navigation guide
- [Market Participants](https://timeswap.gitbook.io/docs/introduction/market-participants.md): There are three main market participants in Timeswap: 1) lenders, 2) borrowers, and 3) liquidity providers (LPs)
- [Lenders](https://timeswap.gitbook.io/docs/introduction/market-participants/lenders.md): Lenders are users who fund Timeswap pools -- to allow borrowers to borrow from -- in return for earning a fixed interest
- [Borrowers](https://timeswap.gitbook.io/docs/introduction/market-participants/borrowers.md): Borrowers are users who borrow from Timeswap pools -- where liquidity is seeded by lenders and liquidity providers -- without needing to manage their positions (as there are no liquidations) in return
- [Liquidity Providers (LPs)](https://timeswap.gitbook.io/docs/introduction/market-participants/lp.md): Liquidity providers (LPs) are users who provide liquidity to Timeswap pools -- to act as the counterparty to lenders and borrowers -- in return for transaction fees
